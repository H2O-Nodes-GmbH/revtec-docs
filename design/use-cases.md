# Use Cases

Holding revSOL or issSOL — or using them in yield-trading protocols — unlocks a range of strategies for SOL stakers and traders:

## Earn REV staking yield with revSOL

revSOL suits SOL holders who expect Solana’s on-chain activity and REV yield to grow. Holding revSOL gives direct exposure to that upside, with REV rewards concentrating faster than in ordinary staking.

Example: _Bob holds SOL and expects chain usage and REV to rise. Instead of ordinary staking, he holds only revSOL. If he is right, he earns more than default staking; if he is wrong, he still earns a yield, just less than ordinary staking would have paid._

## Prioritize stable staking yield with issSOL

issSOL provides exposure to issuance-based yield, which is relatively predictable and declines gradually with Solana’s inflation schedule (assuming the share of SOL that is staked stays roughly stable).

Example: _Chris manages a SOL treasury and wants yield with less uncertainty about near-term earnings. Unsure how market activity will evolve, Chris holds issSOL for a more stable annualized yield._

## Farm REV yield with Exponent YT-revSOL

On [Exponent Finance](https://exponent.finance/), you can exchange revSOL for **YT-revSOL** (yield tokens), increasing exposure to revSOL’s yield — with profit or loss depending on how that yield compares to what you paid for the yield tokens.

Example: _Tom wants to bet on rising Solana activity. He buys as much YT-revSOL as he can on Exponent. If REV rises enough, yield earned can exceed what he paid for the yield tokens (a profit). If not, he can lose money relative to that entry cost._

## Fix REV yield with Exponent PT-revSOL

Yield-trading protocols like Exponent also let you lock in a more fixed yield until maturity, reducing exposure to yield swings.

Example: _Alice sees heavy trading and high REV today, but doubts it will last. She buys **PT-revSOL** (principal tokens) on Exponent, which typically trade at a discount to revSOL because they do not earn yield until maturity. At maturity, PT-revSOL redeems 1:1 into revSOL — the discount path is the “fixed” yield over that period._

## Customize yield exposure

By mixing revSOL and issSOL, users can match their outlook: more REV / fee volatility on one side, steadier issuance on the other.
