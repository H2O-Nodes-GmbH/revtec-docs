# Vision

## Short Term: Volatile, Increasing REV&#x20;

In the short term, REV will remain volatile, mirroring changes in market activity, creating unique opportunities for revSOL traders and yield farmers. Over time, Solana will solidify its dominance as the #1 chain for high volume financial activity, and continue to increasing the production of REV.

## Medium Term: Major Catalysts

Two events will serve as major catalysts for RevTec's users and the evolution of the staking landscape on Solana.&#x20;

* **SIMD-123 Implementation:** The implementation of SIMD-123 on mainnet will normalize the sharing of block rewards from validators to stakers, creating a large-scale awareness of the wealth of block rewards that can be passed to stakers. Validators will have to reassess their fee splits to remain competitive, generally lowering their fees and increasing APYs for stakers.
* **Issuance reduction:** Eventually, the Solana community will succeed in reducing issuance, voting on the spiritual successor to SIMD-228. As a result, REV will become a much larger share of the default Solana staking mix. The staking mix will become much more volatile as a result, making stakers aware of the dynamic nature of REV and aligning them with the ongoing growth of Solana’s on-chain activity and REV.&#x20;

These two events will significantly change the staking landscape and bring widespread attention to REV. RevTec will offer clear, differentiated LSTs based on staker preferences, with competitive fees.

## Long Term: New Investor Classes

RevTec is the first step toward offering differentiated yield products built on staking yield. Staking yield is the largest yield in crypto, by far, but is unpalatable for yield investors due to its volatility, lack of transparency, and lack of differentiation. RevTec's two LSTs are the building blocks upon which pure yield products and [fixed income](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1eiU8qxOxyZZ0D295bFGt8RVw2M8R0jOz/view?usp=sharing) products can be built. When combined with hedging, dollar-denominated yield products can be built at billion-dollar scale, attracting a new class of investors to Solana and boosting participation in its capital markets.&#x20;
