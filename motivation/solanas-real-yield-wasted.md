# Solana's Real Yield, Wasted

Solana is winning: it produced the most real economic value (REV) of any blockchain every month from October 2024 through May 2025. That means real users paying real money to transact. In 2024 it produced over $1.4B, and in the first half of 2025 already over $1B.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (5).png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Monthly REV produced. <br>(Solana in green, Ethereum in purple.) <br>Source: <a href="https://blockworks.co/analytics/chain-comparison">Blockworks Research</a></p></figcaption></figure>

In theory, this REV should flow to stakers. As the next section explains, they only receive part of it — and that share is heavily diluted by inflationary issuance of new SOL.

Until RevTec, there was no efficient way for stakers to collect revenue from Solana’s on-chain economy. As revenues grow (for example from trading volume and stablecoin activity), stakers need a path to align their capital with that growth.
